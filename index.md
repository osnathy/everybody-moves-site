# Everybody Moves

A puzzle game you play with one thumb.

When you swipe, all the circles move at once, and each one moves its own way. A
circle marked 1 goes one square, a circle marked 2 goes two, and the arrow
slides until it hits something.

Get a circle to its home and it stays there for good. It stops moving with the
others and gets in their way instead, and sometimes that's exactly what you
need.

It's in beta on TestFlight right now.
