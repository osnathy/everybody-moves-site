# Support

Found a bug, got stuck, or just want to tell me what you think? Email me at
osnathy83@gmail.com.

If you're testing on TestFlight, a screenshot is the quickest way. Take one and
TestFlight will offer to send it to me with a note.

## Common questions

**I swiped and nothing happened.**
Usually that means nothing could move that way. Every circle was up against a
wall, the edge or another circle, so try a different direction. It also happens
if you swipe before the circles have stopped moving. Wait for them to stop,
then swipe. A swipe that moves nothing doesn't count as a move.

**I'm stuck.**
Undo takes back your last swipe. Restart puts the board back to the start, and
after a restart you'll see a Hint button if you want one.

**Where is my progress saved?**
Only on your phone. If you delete the app, your progress goes with it, unless
your phone's backup brings it back when you reinstall.
