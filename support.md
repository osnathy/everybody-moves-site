# Support

Found a bug, got stuck, or just want to tell me what you think? Email me at
osnathy83@gmail.com.

Tell me which board you were on and what you expected to happen. A
screenshot helps too. If you're testing on TestFlight, take one and TestFlight
will offer to send it to me with a note.

## Common questions

**I swiped and nothing happened.**
Usually that means nothing could move that way. Every circle was up against a
wall, the edge or another circle, so try a different direction. It also happens
if you swipe before the circles have stopped moving. Wait for them to stop,
then swipe. A swipe that moves nothing doesn't count as a move.

**I'm stuck.**
Undo takes back your last swipe. Restart puts the board back to the start. Once
the game can tell you're stuck, for example after a restart, a Hint button
appears. It won't tell you anything until you press it.

**Where is my progress saved?**
Only on your phone. If you delete the app, your progress goes with it. On
iPhone, reinstalling from an iCloud backup brings it back.
