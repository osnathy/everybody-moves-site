# Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026.

Everybody Moves doesn't collect your personal information. You don't need an
account to play, and the game has no ads or tracking.

## What stays on your phone

The game keeps your progress and settings on your phone. It also keeps a record
of how each board went: how long it took, your swipes, undos and restarts, and
whether you asked for a hint. None of this leaves your phone on its own, and
deleting the app deletes it.

## Sending me your play record

If you're testing the game, I may ask you to send me that record. You do it
yourself: open Settings, tap Share capture, and choose how to send it, for
example by email. The file has no name, account or phone id in it.

## Game updates

The game gets its updates through Expo, the service it's built with. When it
checks for one, it tells Expo which version you have and whether you're on
iPhone or Android. It also sends a random code the game makes the first time
you open it, so Expo can recognise the same copy of the game next time.

That code isn't linked to your name, your phone number or anything else about
your phone, and I don't use it to track you. Expo's privacy policy is at
[expo.dev/privacy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Children

The game is suitable for any age, and it doesn't collect personal information
from anyone, children included.
