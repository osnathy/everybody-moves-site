# Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Everybody Moves is made by Osnat Haj Yahia, and I am responsible for this
policy. You can reach me at osnathy83@gmail.com.

The game doesn't ask for your name, your email or an account, and I don't run a
server that stores anything about you. It has no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. The only thing it sends anywhere is a check for game updates, which
is described below.

## What stays on your phone

The game keeps your progress on your phone. That means which boards you've
finished, the fewest moves you've finished each one in and the swipes of that
run, whether your first finish of each board was in the fewest moves, which
boards you picked up a candy on, and a few things the game has
already shown you once, so it doesn't show them again. Your stars aren't stored
separately. The game works them out from that progress. It also keeps your
settings for sound, vibration and motion.

It also keeps a record of how each board went: when you started playing, how
long each board took, your swipes, undos and restarts, and whether you asked
for a hint. None of this leaves your phone on its own.

The game doesn't use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location
services.

If you delete the app, all of this goes with it. If your phone backs up its apps to iCloud or to
Google, the backup can include it, and reinstalling from that backup brings it
back.

## Sending me your play record

If you're testing the game, I may ask you to send me that record. Testing
builds have a Share capture button in Settings. If you press it, you choose
where the record goes, for example an email to me. Nothing is sent unless you
do this. The file has no name, account, phone id or advertising id in it.

In the version from the App Store the button is hidden, and the record stays
on your phone. If you ask me for help, I may show you how to open it. It
still sends nothing unless you press it.

## Game updates

The game gets its updates through Expo, the service it's built with. When it
checks for one, it tells Expo which version you have and whether you're on
iPhone or Android. It also sends a random code the game makes the first time
you open it, so Expo can recognise the same copy of the game next time. Like
any request over the internet, the check reaches Expo from your internet
address.

That code isn't linked to your name, your phone number or anything else about
your phone, and I don't use it to track you. Expo's privacy policy is at
[expo.dev/privacy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Ads in a later version

This version shows no ads. A later version may show ads from Google. Before
any version with ads is released, I'll change this page to say what Google
collects to show them and how you can limit it, and I'll change the date at
the top.

## Children

The game is meant for people aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children
under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices and your data

Everything the game keeps is on your phone, and you can delete it by deleting
the app. I hold no copy, so there is nothing of yours on my side to hand over
or delete. If you have a question about your data, email me.

## If this changes

I'll update this page first and change the date at the top.
